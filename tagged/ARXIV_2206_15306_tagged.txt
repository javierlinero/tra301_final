<TASK> Summarize this abstract for high-school audience while highlight importance of contributions </TASK> <ABSTRACT> <BACKGROUND>Recent work on deep learning for tabular data demonstrates the strong performance of deep tabular models, often bridging the gap between gradient boosted decision trees and neural networks.</BACKGROUND> <BACKGROUND>Accuracy aside, a major advantage of neural models is that they learn reusable features and are easily fine-tuned in new domains.</BACKGROUND> <CONTRIBUTION>This property is often exploited in computer vision and natural language applications, where transfer learning is indispensable when task-specific training data is scarce.</CONTRIBUTION> <CONTRIBUTION>In this work, we demonstrate that upstream data gives tabular neural networks a decisive advantage over widely used GBDT models.</CONTRIBUTION> <CONTRIBUTION>We propose a realistic medical diagnosis benchmark for tabular transfer learning, and we present a how-to guide for using upstream data to boost performance with a variety of tabular neural network architectures.</CONTRIBUTION> <CONTRIBUTION>Finally, we propose a pseudo-feature method for cases where the upstream and downstream feature sets differ, a tabular-specific problem widespread in real-world applications.</CONTRIBUTION> <CONTRIBUTION>Our code is available at https://github.com/LevinRoman/tabular-transfer-learning .</CONTRIBUTION> </ABSTRACT>
