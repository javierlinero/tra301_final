<TASK> Summarize this abstract for high-school audience while highlight importance of contributions </TASK> <ABSTRACT> <BACKGROUND>In multimedia applications, the text and image components in a web document form a pairwise constraint that potentially indicates the same semantic concept.</BACKGROUND> <BACKGROUND>This paper studies cross-modal learning via the pairwise constraint and aims to find the common structure hidden in different modalities.</BACKGROUND> <BACKGROUND>We first propose a compound regularization framework to address the pairwise constraint, which can be used as a general platform for developing cross-modal algorithms.</BACKGROUND> <CONTRIBUTION>For unsupervised learning, we propose a multi-modal subspace clustering method to learn a common structure for different modalities.</CONTRIBUTION> <BACKGROUND>For supervised learning, to reduce the semantic gap and the outliers in pairwise constraints, we propose a cross-modal matching method based on compound ℓ21 regularization.</BACKGROUND> <BACKGROUND>Extensive experiments demonstrate the benefits of joint text and image modeling with semantically induced pairwise constraints, and they show that the proposed cross-modal methods can further reduce the semantic gap between different modalities and improve the clustering/matching accuracy.</BACKGROUND> </ABSTRACT>
