<TASK> Summarize this abstract for high-school audience while highlight importance of contributions </TASK> <ABSTRACT> <BACKGROUND>We study the positivity and asymptotic behaviour of nonnegative solutions of a general nonlocal fast diffusion equation, \[\partial_t u + \mathcal{L}\varphi(u) = 0,\] and the interplay between these two properties.</BACKGROUND> <BACKGROUND>Here $\mathcal{L}$ is a stable-like operator and $\varphi$ is a singular nonlinearity.</BACKGROUND> <CONTRIBUTION>We start by analysing positivity by means of a weak Harnack inequality satisfied by a related elliptic (nonlocal) equation.</CONTRIBUTION> <BACKGROUND>Then we use this positivity to establish the asymptotic behaviour: under certain hypotheses on the nonlocal operator and nonlinearity, our solutions behave asymptotically as the Barenblatt solution of the standard fractional fast diffusion equation.</BACKGROUND> <CONTRIBUTION>The main difficulty stems from the generality of the operator, which does not allow the use of the methods that were available for the fractional Laplacian.</CONTRIBUTION> <CONTRIBUTION>Our results are new even in the case where $\varphi$ is a power.</CONTRIBUTION> </ABSTRACT>
